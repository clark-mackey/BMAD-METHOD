**Source:** https://swiped.co/file/kurobuta-ham-letter-by-gary-bencivenga/
**Client:** The Rosengarten Report
**Subject:** Premium Japanese Kurobuta pork/ham
**Key Elements:**
- Headline: "The Greatest Easter Ham I've Ever Tasted!"
- Subhead: "Outrageously Juicy, Flavorful and Melt-in-Your-Mouth Tender."

- Discovery story format: Author's personal journey discovering the product
- Expert testimonials from famous chefs and food writers
- Sensory language: "moist, tender and almost buttery, with a bright, fresh taste"
